Your photos. Your cover story. A local, offline cover art editor with six panel layouts, six film grades, four one-click presets, and original cinematic lettering.

- Upload one or several photos; select each panel, drag to reframe, zoom, or use face / center / left / right crops.
- Set your caption and subtitle in two bundled OFL display fonts. Adjust outline, tracking, gradient, shadow, uppercase, frame, and vignette.
- Export PNG at 1920 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, or 1080 × 1080. Photos stay on your device; no runtime network requests.

Run `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. No installation or build step is needed.

Built for [Make GTA 6 Cover Art](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).
